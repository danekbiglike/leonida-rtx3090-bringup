# From the prison hallway to the interview with a minimum indirect light level

Lucia walked down the hallway with the officer and reached the seated interview. In the 120 seconds following a manual capture of both characters facing the bars ahead, the renderer presented 2,081 frames, averaging **17.34 FPS**. Five-second windows ranged from **11.2 to 22.0 FPS**. The 95th-percentile frame interval was 90.1 ms and the longest was 174.9 ms. The clock starts when the hallway PNG was saved. We have no camera capture for the exact end of this interval. The automatic marker fired **21.29 seconds later**, after Lucia entered the dayroom. From that marker the next two minutes averaged **17.36 FPS**, with **12.4 FPS** in the slowest five seconds and one interval of **606 ms**. The end image shows both characters at the counselor's desk. These two measurements overlap. Neither reproduces the exact A-102 starting position of the previous **17.40 FPS** result. The dayroom picture had been included among the driver's gate references. We removed it from that set and now recognize it as a separate location. The revised check correctly classified the four stored A-102 starts from the ordered-copy, indexed-write, row-copy and worker-copy runs, and the dayroom start in this run. The saved frames allow the classifications to be reviewed independently. [Start classification](start-location.json) · [Route in four pictures](prison-to-interview.jpg)

Lucia's profile and the door details were visible in the corridor. In the office, light reaches her face and the photograph on the desk is visible. In the dayroom her shirt is faintly visible but the figures remain heavily shadowed in brown haze. The different camera positions prevent isolating the light floor's effect. The captures also cannot establish that the intermittent square highlights disappeared. [Interview at the end of the dayroom interval](interview.jpg) · [Later views](camera.jpg)

The separate timed cell interval ended in the hallway. It averaged **11.30 FPS**, with **5.6 FPS** in the slowest five seconds. GPU utilization had a 97 percent median in both automated intervals. Minimum available physical memory was 5.14 GiB in the cell interval and 4.36 GiB in the dayroom interval. The renderer closed after capture. RTX 3090, Ryzen 7 3700X, SSD, Series X rendering settings and 3840 × 2160 output with no upscaling override or frame generation. This build combines ordered buffer copies, the indirect-light minimum of 0.05, depth reuse with a 16 × 2 gather, and the existing vectorized changed-word comparison at two additional call sites. These combined changes and different scene timings prevent assigning an FPS gain to a single change. Renderer SHA-256 `f27e9413ba54e164ac48421ee0ae034cd35b47b4bf8d567ea43b8e9622c8526c`.
