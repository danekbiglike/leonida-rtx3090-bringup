# RTX 3090 at gate A-102 with depth publication and a read-only memory audit

Lucia followed the officer from the A-102 sign into the dayroom. Over the 120 seconds starting at the gate, the renderer presented **2,114 frames, or 17.62 FPS**. The slowest five seconds averaged **12.4 FPS**, the fastest five seconds averaged **19.8 FPS**, and the longest frame took 219 ms. The comparable ordered-upload trial averaged 17.40 FPS and held 13.6 FPS in its slowest five seconds. The difference of 26 frames across two minutes and the lower minimum do not establish a speed improvement. The trials used 14 and 16 GiB working-set ceilings respectively. [Paired start and end frames](compared-endpoints.jpg) · [Current frame times](escort-frames.csv) · [Previous trial](../4k-prison-ordered-uploads)

The two-minute sample beginning in the cell averaged **15.53 FPS**, with 9.8 in the slowest five seconds and one interval of 1.61 seconds. At its endpoint Lucia was still by the furniture. The previous cell run had already reached the corridor at that time. These cell averages cover different movement. At the end of the gate sample the people in the dayroom still appeared as dark silhouettes. Later, Lucia and the officer reached a bright corridor and her face was visible when the camera turned. The camera did not capture the desk interview during the follow-up. The GPU fixture for this depth change reproduces a lighting error, but the dayroom still has that defect. [Cell frames](cell-frames.csv) · [In-game scenes](compared-endpoints.jpg) · [Later camera angles](camera.jpg) · [Hardware lighting test](../depth-before-lighting-hardware-readback)

During complete telemetry intervals inside the cell and gate samples, the renderer compared **804,816** and **1,324,512** whole read-only pieces respectively and saw zero changed pieces or missing compare entries in either sample. These are repeat observations, including recurring addresses. No boundary-straddling pieces were observed. This control run kept the original write declarations active. The counts support a subsequent clipping trial on this route. The mapped-memory read probe caught eight completed calls over 100 ms inside the cell interval. Each copied 64 KiB. The longest took **1.567302 seconds in the memory-copy portion**. For that call the lookup and mapped-window setup each registered zero whole microseconds. The measurement includes any time the worker was descheduled or waiting for memory during the copy. It cannot distinguish the source page from the destination. [Per-call timings](slow-buffers.csv)

Ryzen 7 3700X, RTX 3090, Series X profile, SSD, 3840 × 2160 output, DLSS and frame generation disabled. Windows had at least 4.72 GiB physical memory available throughout the gate sample. This release also includes the shader waterfall change and the existing 0.05 minimum indirect light setting. Renderer SHA-256 was `95f7a9d38077c6d9f18ad8708fa9ddd53ff3686e1e24f3a47b21d5ecc27e0026`.
