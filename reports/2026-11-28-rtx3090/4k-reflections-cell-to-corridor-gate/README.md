# RTX 3090 at the prison gate with bounded indoor reflections

Lucia started at the open cell door with the officer and reached the barred corridor gate. Over 120 seconds the Series X 4K run presented 1,918 frames, averaging **15.98 FPS**. The first minute averaged 13.85 FPS and the second 18.12 FPS. The slowest five seconds averaged 8.2 FPS, the fastest 20.4. The 95th-percentile frame took 93.38 ms and the longest 1.54 s. No five-second window reached 25 FPS. Forward input was replayed without steering or captures during the timed interval. The ending view faces a gate, so the average includes time spent near it. [Boundary captures](route.jpg) · [Frames](frames.csv) · [Timing and memory](metrics.json)

The corrected launcher supplied both reflection resolve names. The renderer recorded at least **5,000** executions of the reflection limiter by the end of the run. In the preceding launch I supplied the strength and omitted the names, and the log reported that the pass was inactive. That launch ended in a guest null write during the opening, with a lowest available physical memory sample of 4.91 GiB. It produced no comparable corridor sample. Neither capture pair isolates the visual effect on eyes, glass, or the earlier stripe across Lucia's face at the interview. [Corrected settings](settings.json) · [Runtime events](runtime-events.txt) · [Upstream reflection pass](https://github.com/trustno1-coder/VIctory-NVIDIA/pull/818)

During two minutes earlier in the cell, including camera turns, interaction attempts and capture, the average was 9.88 FPS. The longest gap was 7.02 s and one five-second window contained no frames. The earlier prefetch limit engaged at 4,581 MiB of free physical RAM. Available physical memory stayed at or above 5.18 GiB during the later escort window. The game closed at the end of the measurement. [Cell boundaries](cell-route.jpg) · [Cell timings](cell-metrics.json) · [Cell frames](cell-frames.csv)

The submitting thread's median sampled time was 54.9 ms per frame, including 16.8 ms in buffer refresh. The card spent a median 13.7 ms with an empty application queue while that thread was working. These times overlap. The mean board power was 198.65 W. [Submission measurements](submission.csv) · [GPU samples](gpu.csv) · [Pipeline reports](pipelines.csv)

This route ends earlier than the [16.18 FPS 4K run that reached the interview](../4k-guard-escort-and-interview). These runs do not isolate the speed effect of a single change.
