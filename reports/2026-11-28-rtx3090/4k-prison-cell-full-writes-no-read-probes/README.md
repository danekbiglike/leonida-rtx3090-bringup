# Prison cell with mapped-read timers and the read-only audit disabled

The RTX 3090 presented **1,139 frames in 120 seconds, or 9.49 FPS**, at 3840 × 2160 output with the Series X profile. Lucia was still in the cell at the end. Three complete five-second windows contained zero presents. The longest time between two presents was **17.210 seconds**, from 1.808 to 19.018 seconds after the cell marker. The second minute averaged 13.2 FPS. The input driver subsequently tried to approach the door but did not capture an A-102 start. [Complete metrics](cell-metrics.json) · [Frame times](cell-frames.csv) · [Cell endpoints](cell-route.jpg)

The launcher kept full GPU write declarations and disabled both the read-only comparison audit and the slow mapped-view read timer. The renderer used the original 160 × 90 lighting grid. The stall still occurred. Windows reported five process hard faults in the 16 seconds sampled inside the longest frame, roughly 0.94 MiB of process reads, and at least 4.54 GiB of available physical memory in that frame. These counters cannot identify the blocked operation. The renderer resumed, completed the cell interval and closed at the end of the test.
