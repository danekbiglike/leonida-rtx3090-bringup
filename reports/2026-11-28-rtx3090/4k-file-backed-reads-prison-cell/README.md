# File-backed reads during the RTX 3090 prison-cell trial

With small GPU buffers allocated from pooled heaps, the renderer presented **1,372 frames in 120 seconds (11.43 FPS)** in the prison cell. The slowest five seconds averaged **0.8 FPS**. The 95th-percentile frame took 120.7 ms and the longest took 6.360 seconds. The player remained inside the cell. The run ended through its normal test stop and supplied no A-102 escort score. The [prior renderer](../4k-prison-depth-and-write-audit) averaged 15.53 FPS in its cell sample and 17.62 FPS on the two-minute escort. Median cell draw counts were 2,170 in that earlier run and 5,510 here. The camera and workload differed. The [measured allocation speedup](../concurrent-buffer-placement-on-3090) has yet to yield a demonstrated in-game gain. The play launcher keeps the prior configuration. All 13 mapped-view copies logged above the 100 ms threshold during the cell interval transferred exactly 64 KiB from the emulator's **file-backed guest-memory pool**. The longest copy took 4.570 seconds after resolving the mapping. The timer brackets a normal memory copy and includes any period the thread waited or was descheduled. It does not locate the wait on the source, on the destination, or on a physical drive. [Per-read timers](slow-buffers.csv)

In the 6.360-second frame, four observed copies on three workers covered **6.289 seconds** when overlapping intervals were counted only once. Up to three copies were outstanding simultaneously. Process hard faults increased by four over 5.998 seconds covered by counter samples inside that frame. A separate 4.672-second frame included one 4.570-second copy and zero additional hard faults over the 3.001 seconds bracketed by samples. The counters cover the whole process and the sampled parts of the frames. [Read overlap and fault counts](mapped-read-frames.csv)

Ryzen 7 3700X, RTX 3090, 32 GiB RAM, SSD, Series X profile, 3840 × 2160 output, frame generation and DLSS disabled. Minimum available physical memory during the scored interval was 4.37 GiB.
