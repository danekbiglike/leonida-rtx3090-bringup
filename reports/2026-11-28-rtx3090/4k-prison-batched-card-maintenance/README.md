# Combining maintenance dispatches on an RTX 3090

The renderer presented 1,338 frames during the two minutes from the cell to the corridor, averaging **11.15 FPS**. The slowest five seconds averaged **5.6 FPS**, the 95th-percentile frame interval was 123.9 ms and the longest was 2.022 s. None of the 24 five-second windows reached 25 FPS. The ordered-upload build previously measured 11.87 FPS in this part of the game under a different guest-memory limit. This single trial shows no improvement in cell throughput. Across 111 paired samples the median per-frame workload was 180 hash passes and 358 comparisons. Batching issued medians of **10 and 20 native GPU dispatches** respectively for that work. Individual samples retain the duplicate-case fallback counts. On the physical RTX 3090 the hardware fixture checked complete output flags and hashes in seven observations involving COPY and UAV producers, pending work and duplicate handling. It ran on the retail D3D12 device with the debug layer disabled. The patch includes the local slow-read probes.
