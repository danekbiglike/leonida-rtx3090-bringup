# Where committed memory grew after the 4K prison walk

The personal-save foreground player continued presenting for at least 20 minutes. Windows reported a 36.76 GiB job peak against a 42 GiB process limit and no limit events through minute 20. The last minute presented 144 frames. During the test the scene changed and a compiler started after the timed two-minute walk. The minute-by-minute frame counts cover loading and cutscenes as well as play. [20-minute counters](minutes.csv) · [Summary](metrics.json) · [Earlier timed walk](https://github.com/danekbiglike/leonida-rtx3090-bringup/tree/34db17ef224ae873c71e192c2a66b9c693314ad4/reports/2026-11-27-rtx3090/ordinary-uploads-ssd-pool-player)

In a 150-second interval after the walk, private committed address-space regions grew by 202.88 MiB. Of that increase, 159.08 MiB was in host write-combined memory, 27.54 MiB was in host allocations with a 4 MiB extent, and 15.48 MiB was in allocations with an extent of 0xfd0000 bytes. Console-address private regions changed by one 4 KiB page. The independently grouped 180-second sample recorded 235.12 MiB of private growth. Across those endpoints, bases present only in the final snapshot accounted for 199.63 MiB, bases absent at the end for 12.02 MiB, and growth at bases present in both for 47.51 MiB. A base address may be reused between scans. [150-second categories](kinds.csv) · [180-second allocation groups](regions.csv)

A later 60-second interval saw a net 94.05 MiB increase in the host write-combined category. The allocations with a 64 MiB extent accounted for a net 64.00 MiB. The 32 MiB extent group added 14.94 MiB of committed pages. Extent groups describe allocation size, not a resource owner. The Rust compiler was active during this last interval. All figures are specific to this VIctory process. More scenes and allocation-lifetime data are needed to distinguish a persistent leak from retained buffers. [Size groups](writecombined-sizes.csv) · [Matching totals](size-samples.csv)

The read-only sampler uses VirtualQueryEx to walk the verified renderer's address space. It samples region metadata, groups committed bytes by region type, protection and allocation extent, and does not publish raw addresses. The size rules and the 1 TiB console address boundary follow the local renderer's copystat.rs. A scan took approximately two to three seconds and is not an atomic process snapshot. The first two scans series overlap in time and began after the FPS measurement. Process charge, virtual committed region size, physical working set and dedicated GPU memory are different measurements.
