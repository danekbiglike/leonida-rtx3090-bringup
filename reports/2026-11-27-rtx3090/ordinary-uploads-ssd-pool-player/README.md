# Personal-save 4K prison walk with ordinary texture uploads

On 28 November 2026 in Moscow, the RTX 3090 and Ryzen 7 3700X presented 708 frames in 120 seconds, averaging 5.90 FPS. The slowest aligned five-second interval averaged 2.8 FPS. The 95th percentile frame interval was 205.43 ms and the longest interval was 5.012 s. The first minute was spent at the open cell door with the officer. During the second minute Lucia walked out of the cell and reached the barred gate with the officer. Extra rightward movement, camera adjustments and forward inputs were needed to clear the table. The three images show the player's actual window. [Frames](frames.csv) · [Route images](route.jpg) · [Manual inputs during measurement](steering.csv)

The run used the normal player shortcut, persistent save and pipeline cache, D: game installation, a 6,538,919,936-byte SSD-backed guest memory pool, Series X graphics settings and 3840 × 2160 output. We tested ordinary texture uploads and disabled card steps. There were eight command-list translation workers. No compiler was active during the measurement. All 21 pipeline samples reported zero pipelines building. The foreground window belonged to the renderer when the conversation input was sent. Visible lighting artifacts persisted. [Present and memory summary](metrics.json) · [GPU samples](gpu.csv)

Across 103 paired synchronization reports, the median submitting-thread wait for the GPU was 84.1 ms per frame. GPU-side buffer comparisons read a median 1,148 MB per frame, with 17,518 64-KiB pieces compared and 1,581 reported changed. In the earlier walk with the same renderer and background texture uploads plus card steps, these figures were 16.5 ms, 522 MB, 7,966 pieces and 1,596 changed. The trials had different time at the door, cache histories, draw counts and more than one configuration difference. The counters identify where the time went in each run. They do not isolate a single switch. The renderer's preparation, submit waits and relay timing overlap. The median reported preparation was 67.5 ms, including 31.8 ms for buffer copies and 1.2 ms for textures. Median sampled GPU utilization was 63%. Private committed memory grew from 36,963,008,512 to 37,969,182,720 bytes. During the longest frame gap, overlap-weighted one-second counters estimated 143 renderer hard faults, with 1.73 MiB read on the physical disk hosting D:, 31.89 MiB on C: and 1.41 MiB on the Windows paging disk.
